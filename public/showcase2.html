How It Works

Four steps from raw data to insights

The pipeline runs automatically on a schedule or on demand, with no intervention needed.

1

Data Ingestion

The system pulls data from multiple sources — Google Sheets, CRM exports, and third-party APIs — and consolidates it into a central processing layer.

2

Processing & Transformation

Apps Script processes the raw data: validating entries, calculating KPIs, applying business logic (tier thresholds, date ranges, eligibility rules), and enriching records.

3

Storage & Warehousing

Processed data is written to BigQuery for scalable storage and historical tracking, enabling trend analysis and year-over-year comparison.

4

Dashboard & Report Distribution

Looker Studio dashboards update automatically. HTML reports with Chart.js visualizations are generated and distributed to stakeholders via Google Drive or email.
