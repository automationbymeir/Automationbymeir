Web Development

Plenty of people will sell you a website; fewer will build one wired into how your business actually runs. I build fast, maintainable sites and web apps — from a marketing landing page to an application with user accounts and a real backend — that connect directly to the tools behind your operations.

On the front end I work with React, or plain fast HTML when that's all the project needs — no framework for framework's sake. Backends run on Node.js or Firebase, with authentication and data access handled through standard, boring, secure practices.

Google Apps Script Flows

Much of my work lives inside Google Workspace. With Apps Script I connect Gmail, Drive, Sheets, and Calendar into flows your site can trigger directly — a form submission that parses into a Sheet, generates a Doc, and notifies your team in Chat, with error handling and logging built in from the start.

Power Automate Flows

For Microsoft shops I build Power Automate flows that tie into Office 365, SharePoint, and Teams — onboarding sequences, approval chains, SharePoint lists kept in sync with your databases. Low-code where that keeps things maintainable, custom code where the logic demands it.

Every project ships with the full source code in your hands and documentation that explains how it fits together. You get a fixed-price proposal before I start, Loom and WhatsApp progress updates during the build, and a 14-day fix guarantee after launch.
