AI-Powered Systems

Most "AI chatbots" fail because they don't know your business. I build AI agents wired into your actual documents, tools, and rules — so they draft the email, fill the report, and trigger the next step correctly instead of guessing.

Under the hood, I connect the Claude, GPT, or Gemini APIs to the systems you already run: Google Workspace, Slack, Monday.com, Firebase, Power Automate. The model gets retrieval access to your real documents and data, so answers come from your price list and your policies — not from the open internet.

Typical builds: an inbox agent that classifies incoming requests and drafts replies for your approval, a quoting assistant that pulls line items from Sheets and produces a ready-to-send PDF, or a support triage bot that answers from your docs and hands off to a human when it isn't sure.

I'm deliberate about what the AI should not do. Anything irreversible — sending, paying, deleting — goes through a human approval step, and every model call is logged so you can see exactly what the agent did and why. I won't promise self-improving models or fine-tuned magic; I promise a system you can inspect and trust.

You get a fixed-price proposal before work starts, Loom and WhatsApp progress updates while I build, full ownership of the source code at handover, and a 14-day fix guarantee after delivery.

If a repetitive, judgment-light task is eating your week, book a free 30-minute consultation or email me at service@automationbymeir.com — I'll tell you honestly whether AI is the right tool for it.
