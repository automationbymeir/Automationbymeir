Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to change the tools my team already uses?

No. I build automations on top of the tools you already have — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Monday.com, Slack, your CRM. The goal is to connect them, not replace them.

What happens if an automation breaks?

If a delivered automation doesn't work as specified, I fix it free within 14 days of delivery. Beyond that, ongoing support and monitoring options are available — you're never left with a black box.

Why not just build it myself with Zapier or Make?

For simple two-app triggers, you should! I come in when workflows have logic, edge cases, approvals, documents, or data volume that off-the-shelf connectors handle poorly — and when you want it built once, correctly, with source code you own.

How much does a project cost?

Packages start at $300; most custom projects range $900–$5,000. Every project gets a fixed-price proposal up front — no hourly surprises.

How do payments work?

Through PayPal or Fiverr, per the proposal. Packages are listed on the packages page; custom projects are invoiced at the agreed fixed price.

Who actually does the work?

I do — one senior consultant, no handoffs, no juniors. You get direct access to the person building your system, with progress updates via Loom, WhatsApp, or email.
