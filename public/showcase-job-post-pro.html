The Problem

Writing job briefs is repetitive, slow, and inconsistent

Every new hire starts the same way — someone interviews a hiring manager, takes notes, then spends an hour turning those notes into a properly formatted brief. Job Post Pro automates the entire process from data collection to final document, ensuring every brief is complete, consistent, and ready to share.

How It Works

Three steps from idea to finished brief

The system captures requirements, generates content with AI, and delivers a formatted Google Doc automatically.

1

Smart Questionnaire

A dynamic web form captures all required job details — role, responsibilities, requirements, compensation, and more. Fields adapt based on previous answers. All responses are auto-saved to browser storage so no progress is ever lost.

2

AI Content Generation

The collected data is sent to OpenAI GPT-3.5 Turbo via Apps Script. The model generates a comprehensive, professionally written brief including job description, requirements, benefits, and a sourcing strategy — all structured according to your template.

3

Google Docs Delivery

The finished brief is automatically written into a new Google Doc, properly formatted and titled. It's organized in the correct Drive folder and ready to share — no copy-pasting, no reformatting.
