Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Who I am

This website, automationbymeir.com ("the Site"), is operated by Meir Horwitz, doing business as Automation by Meir. For any privacy question, contact service@automationbymeir.com.

What data is collected

  • Contact form and scheduler: when you send a message or book a consultation, I collect the name, email address, project details, and selected meeting time you provide. This data is used only to respond to your inquiry and manage the meeting.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity collect usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type, interaction heatmaps) to help improve the Site. If you decline, these tools are not loaded at all.
  • Payment: payments are processed by PayPal. I never see or store your card details; PayPal's own privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Cookies

The Site sets a single functional item in your browser's local storage to remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies from Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are set only after you accept. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the Site's data in your browser.

How data is stored and shared

Form and scheduling data is stored in Google Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. Data is never sold or shared with third parties except the processors named above (Google, Microsoft, PayPal) as needed to operate the Site.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email service@automationbymeir.com and I will respond within 30 days.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
