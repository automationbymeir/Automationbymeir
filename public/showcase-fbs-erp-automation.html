The Challenge

High-volume German B2B inquiries & enterprise purchase orders

As a leading German manufacturer of custom promotional flags, beachflags, and flagpoles serving major enterprises like TÜV NORD, BabyOne, Lidl, and Dehner, FBS Fahnen receives dozens of complex email inquiries and multi-page PDF purchase orders daily. Each required manually searching customer history in Easybill, calculating tiered volume pricing (Staffelpreise) and freight matrices, creating formal ERP documents, and manually tracking open quotes for follow-up.

System Architecture

Three autonomous engines working in concert

Deployed as three isolated, self-healing Docker microservices on a dedicated cloud server, the suite automates the entire B2B sales lifecycle from first inquiry to closed order.

1

Engine 1 — Autonomous AI Quotation Engine (Angebotserstellung)

Monitors the sales inbox 24/7 via IMAP, extracts technical flag specifications (dimensions, materials, finishing/Konfektion, quantities) from German emails and PDF attachments using Multimodal AI, matches or auto-creates the customer in Easybill, calculates historical or tiered pricing plus automated shipping tiers, verifies every line item through a secondary AI Quality Gate (DraftAuditor), and saves a ready-to-send Easybill Quote Draft.

2

Engine 2 — Order Confirmation & Delivery Note Engine (Auftragsbestätigung & Lieferschein)

Detects incoming B2B purchase orders and parses complex PDF order tables. Includes dedicated enterprise rules — such as a 19-SKU catalog engine for TÜV NORD and automatic differing shipping address (Abweichende Lieferadresse) formatting for multi-branch buyers like BabyOne. Generates both the priced Order Confirmation (for 1-click invoice conversion) and the Delivery Note directly in Easybill.

3

Engine 3 — Smart Quote Follow-Up Engine (Angebots-Follow-Up)

Tracks finalized quotes in Easybill after 5 days, automatically verifies that no subsequent order or invoice was created, determines personalized German salutations (Herr / Frau) and product categories (Werbefahnen, Fahnenmasten, Fahnenzubehör), downloads the official Easybill Quote PDF, sends a personalized reminder email with the PDF attached, CCs the sales team, and syncs the sent message to the IMAP Sent folder.

Intelligent Pricing & Order Routing

How the AI handles quotes vs. enterprise orders

Every incoming document is classified and routed to the dedicated pipeline with strict deterministic guardrails.

Path A — AI Quotation Pipeline

4-tier pricing & quality verification

Customer & Domain Resolution Matches existing Easybill customers by email or corporate domain, or auto-creates a complete profile with address & VAT ID.
Historical & Tiered Pricing (Staffelpreise) Cross-references past quotes/invoices for exact repeat-order pricing or applies volume discount tiers automatically.
Automated Shipping Matrix Calculates exact freight & packaging costs based on flagpole length, weight, and item quantities.
Dual-LLM DraftAuditor Gate Second AI pass audits quantities, unit prices, and specs before creating the Easybill draft and emailing the sales rep.
Path B — Enterprise Order & Follow-Up

PDF purchase orders & automated conversion

Enterprise Catalog Matching Maps customer article numbers and descriptions to exact Easybill SKUs, dimensions, and net unit prices.
Differing Delivery Address Detection Separates billing headquarters from branch or agency delivery addresses automatically on both ERP documents.
Dual Document Generation Creates both the priced CHARGE_CONFIRM (Auftragsbestätigung) and DELIVERY (Lieferschein) drafts in seconds.
5-Day Smart Quote Follow-Up Follows up on open quotes with the original Easybill PDF attached and full IMAP Sent-folder synchronization.
